Privacy policy

rclone-drive is used only by its owner, on the owner's own computer, to read and write files in the owner's own Google Drive.

  • The app accesses Google Drive data only of the account that signs in, and only to copy, list and check the owner's files.
  • No data is collected, stored on any server, sold or shared with anyone. Access tokens stay on the owner's computer.
  • Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Last updated: 26 November 2026.
